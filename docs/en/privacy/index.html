1. Scope

This policy covers the Korean and English iPhone, iPad and Mac versions of 1891: Life Out West / 1891: 서부에서 살아가기 and this support website. The app does not require a separate game account and has no user chat feature.

The publisher for this policy is SupraCore. Privacy and support requests can be sent to support@supracore.co.kr.

2. Information on your device

Progress, your chosen player name, inventory and records, settings, purchase entitlement status and Photo Mode images are stored or processed on your device. You do not need to use your real name. This information supports gameplay, saving progress, applying preferences and checking purchase access.

Use the game’s delete controls and your device’s file and app management tools to manage this information. Photos, recovery copies and operating-system backups may remain separately from the main save files. Device files may be included in operating-system backups.

3. Apple services

When iCloud save sync is enabled on iPhone or iPad, save-slot progress, a summary that may include your chosen player name, save times, device type and certain progress indicators are synchronized through Apple iCloud. You can disable sync in Settings. Disabling it does not delete existing copies. Deleting a slot in the game also deletes its iCloud copy.

On iPhone and iPad, Game Center receives achievements and leaderboard scores when you use it. Basic gameplay is available without Game Center. The Mac version currently does not support iCloud saves or Game Center.

Full Version purchases and restoration are handled through the Apple App Store and Unity IAP. Purchase and transaction information and entitlement status are used in that process. The app does not directly request or store card details. Apple account information, purchase history and Apple service data are handled under Apple’s policies and account controls.

4. Unity engine and purchase SDK

The app uses Unity and Unity IAP 5.4.4. The app has no advertising or advertising tracking feature, and the project’s Unity Analytics and Ads services are disabled. These settings do not mean that all SDK data processing is absent.

Unity’s documentation for IAP 5.4 and later describes the handling of installation and session identifiers, device information, country and purchase-related information. The scope can depend on the store, features and SDK configuration in use. Processing supports purchase functionality, service operation and security, under the relevant Unity policies.

5. Photos and sharing

Photo Mode images are saved on your device. Only when you choose to share are images sent through the system share sheet to your chosen destination. Photo library add permission is used when you save an image to the library. The receiving service handles a shared copy under its own policies.

6. Support requests and website visits

This is a static website with no support submission form, advertising, visitor analytics scripts or cookies of its own. Copying the support template happens in your browser and does not submit it to this website.

When you choose a language, this browser stores only the Korean or English preference for your next visit. The preference is not used for identification, advertising or visitor analytics, and is not sent to an external server. Change it using the language switch or chooser, or remove it by clearing this site’s browser data.

If you email support, your email address, message and any device details or images you choose to attach are used to respond. Messages are kept only for the time needed to respond, then deleted without undue delay. Records required by law are retained separately for the applicable period.

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs and stores visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes, whether or not a visitor is signed in. GitHub handles this access information under its Privacy Statement.

Bug reports in GitHub Issues are public. GitHub account details and posted content are handled under GitHub’s policies. Do not publish email addresses, receipts, save files or other personal information. Send private support or removal requests to the support email. External copies and GitHub’s own records and retention remain subject to that service’s policies.

7. Retention and deletion

On-device information may remain until you delete it or remove it using the app’s or operating system’s storage controls. Purchase history, iCloud and Game Center information, and external service logs are retained according to the relevant provider’s policies and account controls.

Deleting a slot and disabling iCloud sync are different actions. Removing the app does not necessarily remove iCloud copies, images shared elsewhere or operating-system backups.

8. Choices and privacy requests

You can manage iCloud sync and game preferences in Settings, and photo permissions in your device settings. Contact the privacy address above for applicable access, correction, deletion or other privacy rights. Only the minimum information needed to verify a request will be requested.

Use Apple’s controls for Apple accounts, purchases and Apple service data. For information controlled directly by Unity, use the request channels described in Unity’s policy.

9. External services and international processing

Information handled by Apple, Unity and hosting services may be processed or stored outside your country. See each provider’s policy for processing locations, retention and safeguards. The publisher reviews the services and processing actually used in the distribution and updates this policy accordingly.

10. Changes to this policy

When the service or its data practices change, this page and its effective date will be updated. Material changes, such as changes to processing purposes, will be communicated through the appropriate notice process.
