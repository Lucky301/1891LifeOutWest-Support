Frequently asked questions

How much can I play for free?

The first three in-game days of the main journey are free. Starting on in-game day four, the Full Version requires a one-time non-consumable In-App Purchase. This is not a three-day real-time trial or an auto-renewing subscription. The separate weekly challenge allows seven in-game days without a purchase.

Where can I buy or restore the Full Version?

Open Settings from the title screen or pause menu and scroll to the bottom for Purchase and Restore Purchases. Sign in to the App Store with the Apple account used for the purchase and check your internet connection. Restoring a purchase does not restore a save file. Prices are provided by the App Store in your local currency.

I paid, but the Full Version is still locked.

Check your Apple purchase history to confirm the purchase completed, then use Restore Purchases in Settings. A pending approval must be approved before access is granted. If the connection is unstable, try again later. If the issue continues, include your device, app version and the displayed message in your request.

How is my progress saved?

There are three save slots. You can save manually from the pause menu. During play, autosave runs about every 180 seconds when no window is open, and the game also saves when the app pauses. Weekly challenges use a separate file. Before deleting the app, check your device backup and any available iCloud copy.

Can I continue on another device?

On iPhone and iPad, use the same Apple account, iCloud and the in-game iCloud save setting. If another device has a newer save, the title-screen prompt lets you choose a copy. The Mac version currently uses local saves and does not sync progress with iPhone or iPad.

How do I switch between Korean and English?

Open Settings > 언어 · Language and choose 한국어 or English. During play, the game saves and returns to the title screen. Journal entries already written may remain in the language used when they were created.

What are the basic controls?

On iPhone and iPad, drag the left area to move and use the hand button to interact. Use the aim/fire and reload buttons for combat. On Mac: WASD/arrows to move, E to interact, mouse to aim/fire, R to reload, I for inventory, J for journal, M for map and Esc to pause. Gamepads are also supported.

The text is small or the scene looks too busy.

Try the larger UI size and reduced terrain detail in Settings. You can also adjust high-quality graphics, screen shake, reduced flashing, color-vision correction and aim assist. iPhone uses landscape. iPad supports rotation and resizable windows; portrait windows may show bars above and below the game.

Where do Photo Mode pictures go?

Photo Mode images are saved in the app’s on-device storage. On iPhone and iPad, the system share sheet lets you save the image or choose a destination after capture. Photo library permission is used when saving an image to your library. Manage previously shared copies in the service you shared them with.

Does deleting a slot also delete its iCloud copy?

Deleting a slot in the game deletes its main local save files and its iCloud copy. Turning sync off alone does not delete an existing cloud copy. Photos, recovery files and operating-system backups may remain separately. Recovery of deleted progress is not guaranteed.
